# DAWSON WELLS GALLERY — VULNERABILITY DISCLOSURE & SECURITY POLICY
Last updated: October 2026

1. COMMITMENT TO DATA PROTECTION & ART PATRON PRIVACY
Dawson Wells Gallery and its digital architectural partner Clepto (https://www.clepto.in) are committed to maintaining the highest security, privacy, and integrity standards for our collector acquisition portal, digital viewing rooms, and private client communications.

2. REPORTING A VULNERABILITY
If you discover a security vulnerability in our digital systems, we appreciate your responsible and prompt disclosure:
- Email: security@dawsonwellsgallery.com and contact@clepto.in
- Please include:
  * Detailed summary of the potential issue
  * Steps to reproduce (proof of concept, URL, headers)
  * Any relevant log extracts or screenshots
  * Your preferred contact details for coordination

3. OUR COMMITMENT TO REPORTERS
- We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 2 business days.
- We will work diligently to validate and remediate verified issues.
- We will not pursue legal action against security researchers who act in good faith, avoid privacy violations, do not disrupt services, and comply with responsible disclosure guidelines.

4. SCOPE
In-scope:
- Primary web application: https://dawsonwellsdesigns.com
- Collector inquiry workflows and contact APIs
- Image delivery infrastructure

Out-of-scope:
- Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks
- Social engineering, phishing, or physical attacks on gallery personnel or physical premises
- Third-party external services or email transit providers

5. ENCRYPTION & COMPLIANCE
- All transport is strictly enforced via TLS 1.3 / TLS 1.2 with HSTS.
- Content Security Policy (CSP) is actively monitored and enforced.
